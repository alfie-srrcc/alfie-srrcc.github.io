01 / THE PROJECT

One office.
A connected design study.

I developed a lighting layout in DIALux and used Bluebeam to prepare electrical markups and circuit information for the same office building.

The plan includes an open office, meeting rooms, private offices, washrooms, a corridor and support rooms. My work covered luminaire research, calculation review, socket placement and drawing notation.

01

Research luminaires and adjust their quantity to suit the lighting needs of each room.

02

Calculate and review

Read calculation results and false colour plots to examine light levels and their distribution.

03

Draw and communicate

Prepare layouts, circuit references and room schedules that relate to the same plan.

Project context: a design learning exercise, presented using the drawings and software results retained in my notes.

02 / LIGHTING DESIGN

Room by room.
Fitting by fitting.

I researched suitable luminaires and adjusted their quantity in response to room lighting needs, considering both the ceiling construction and the activity below.

Ceiling construction

Distinguishing between suspended and solid ceilings, and the mounting arrangements each requires.

Room conditions

Researching ingress protection for washroom fittings, considering their exposure to water.

Task and atmosphere

Considering lighting above work surfaces, warmer white for washrooms and a cooler white appearance for working areas.

06 / TECHNICAL LEARNING

Understanding
behind the layout.

Power factor

I learnt the distinction between real, reactive and apparent power. For a single phase circuit with sinusoidal voltage and current, using RMS values:

Apparent powerS = VI (VA)

Real powerP = VI cos φ (W)

Power factorP / S = cos φ

φ is the phase difference between voltage and current. At a given real power and voltage, lower power factor means higher current, affecting loading and heating.

Maintenance factor

Lighting performance changes as light sources age and dirt accumulates. The maintenance factor accounts for these effects in the calculation.

Cleaning intervals, ambient conditions and light source performance influence the factor used.

What I take forward

This study helped me connect software results with drawing decisions. Reading the calculations alongside the layout made the relationship between room use, desk locations and light distribution clearer.
